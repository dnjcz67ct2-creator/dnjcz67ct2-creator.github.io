Sean Marquez

6'0" Power Forward • Trojans AAU • Montebello

About Me

Sean Marquez is a 6'0" power forward currently playing for Trojans AAU in Montebello. He has competitive basketball experience with Arellano Mabini and has participated in interbranch basketball competition.

Sean was born on March 28, 2008, and continues to develop his game through competition, teamwork, and AAU basketball.

Achievements

2024

Arellano Mabini — Division Meet Gold Medalist

Sean earned Gold Medalist recognition in the Basketball Competition (Boys) – Division Meet at Arellano University, Apolinario Mabini Campus, Pasay City, in February 2024.

Interbranch

Interbranch Basketball Competition

Sean also participated in the Interbranch Basketball Competition (Boys) at Arellano University – Juan Sumulong Campus. His certificate of recognition documents his participation in the 2024 competition.
